Stem Splitter — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Stem Splitter Android app.

Short version: your audio files and separated stems are processed and stored on your device only — this app never uploads them anywhere. The only data leaving your device is what Google's advertising and billing services collect on their own, described below.

1. Information we collect

Your audio and separated stems

Tracks you import, and the stems Stem Splitter separates them into, are processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning models. This app does not upload your audio to any server, and we do not have access to it. Saved projects are stored in this app's private storage on your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Stem Splitter shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect an advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location (from IP address) to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example, users in the EEA/UK), a consent form is shown before any ads are requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or withdraw consent at any time from within the app.

Purchase data (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never see or store your payment details — those are handled by Google Play.

Device permissions

MicrophoneUsed only while you're actively using the built-in tuner feature. Audio is processed live on-device and is not recorded to a file or transmitted anywhere.
Audio filesUsed to let you pick a track to import. We only read the file you explicitly select.
Internet / network stateUsed by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing to fetch ads and process purchases.

2. Third-party services

This app uses the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — ad serving. How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — consent management for ads.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional in-app purchase.

Google's own privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your choices

  • Ad personalization — you can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device's Google Settings → Ads, or by withdrawing consent through the in-app consent form (where shown).
  • Your audio and saved projects — fully under your control, since they never leave your device. Delete a saved project in-app, or clear the app's data/uninstall it to remove everything at once.
  • Remove ads entirely — the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase stops ad requests going forward.

4. Children's privacy

Stem Splitter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "last updated" date below.
